Runs a small controlled comparison in which university students receive otherwise identical political interviews from AI or human interviewers. Human coding finds broadly similar response quality and comparable overall guideline adherence, but different failure modes: the AI often misses appropriate follow-up opportunities and sometimes gives evaluative encouragement, whereas humans more often miss active-listening behaviors or suggest answers. The study is useful for implementation guidance but its very small student sample makes its comparative estimates proof-of-concept evidence.
