Randomly assigns 40 participants to synchronous text interviews conducted by humans or a locally hosted OpenHermes 2.5 Mistral 7B system using the same interface and interview guide. Human interviewers elicit longer responses per question, while the AI asks questions faster and therefore produces longer interviews overall; coded specificity and relevance do not differ significantly. The design is valuable for holding modality constant and foregrounding privacy and reproducibility, but it compares against non-expert human interviewers, is unblinded, uses a small sample, and does not establish equivalence to high-quality in-depth qualitative interviewing.
