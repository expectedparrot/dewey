Uses a 2-by-2 experiment comparing web versus chatbot survey platforms and formal versus casual conversational style. Chatbot respondents produced more differentiated answers and showed less satisficing, while casual style reduced satisficing only in the chatbot condition; qualitative analysis suggests that the chatbot performed some functions of a human interviewer. This provides early comparative evidence on interface and tone, although it concerns structured survey administration rather than open-ended LLM-led qualitative interviews.
