Develops a simple, adaptable single-agent system for conducting text and voice qualitative interviews at scale. The paper evaluates AI-led interviews against trained human interviewers, respondent assessments, open-text survey responses, and content-quality measures across applications involving meaning in life, political preferences, education and occupation choices, and policy mental models. It reports that short AI-led interviews approach average trained-human quality under comparable online constraints, elicit substantially richer responses than open text fields, and can be deployed across topics and languages. For this review, it is a central evaluation and implementation paper, while its claims should be interpreted within the studied samples, relatively short interview formats, and the authors’ proposed quality metrics.
