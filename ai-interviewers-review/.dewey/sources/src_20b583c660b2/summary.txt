Introduces SmartProbe, a modular LLM-based virtual moderator evaluated through 300 expert-rated generated probes and parallel market-research surveys. Market researchers rated 69% of generated probes highly; respondent answers were rated highly in 76% of SmartProbe cases versus 25% with generic probes. The applied samples are substantial, but proprietary in-house ratings and a bundled nonrandom platform comparison limit causal and independent-validity claims.
