Randomly assigns 78 children aged 6–8 to questions formulated by ChatGPT or naive human interviewers after a witnessed mock event. The LLM asks fewer questions, elicits similar total unique correct information, more correct information per question, and less false information overall, with no condition difference in false information per question. This is a directly eligible AI-versus-human comparison with objective accuracy outcomes, although questions generated by the LLM were delivered by a human and the specialized forensic context should be coded as a distinct subgroup.
