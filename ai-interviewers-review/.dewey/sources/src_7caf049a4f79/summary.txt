Evaluates training with a fine-tuned child-avatar chatbot across four simulated investigative interviews among 30 students. Automated question-type coding showed substantial agreement with human coders (κ=0.80), and the group receiving direct feedback improved recommended open-ended questioning more than the no-feedback group. This is adjacent evidence—the AI is an interviewee and coach, not interviewer—and small groups plus shared lecture exposure limit causal and real-world generalization.
