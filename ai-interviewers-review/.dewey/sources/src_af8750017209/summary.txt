Compares four theory-grounded LLM interview probes—descriptive, idiographic, clarifying, and explanatory—across exploration, requirements, and evaluation tasks in a split-plot experiment with 64 participants. Probe type and research stage affect Gricean response-quality measures and user experience, providing experimentally controlled evidence about which adaptive follow-ups work rather than merely whether a chatbot interface is present. It is eligible for outcome-level synthesis, but multiple effects share the same participants and must be modeled as dependent.
