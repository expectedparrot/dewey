Introduces CommunityBots, a multi-agent chatbot platform for eliciting public input across household, work, and health domains. Following a Wizard-of-Oz design study, the authors compare the multi-agent platform with a single-agent baseline using 96 crowd workers and report greater engagement, higher-quality responses, fewer conversational interruptions, and helpful interface cues. It broadens the review from one-interviewer systems to division of interviewing roles across agents, though it is not an LLM system and its evaluation uses a specific public-input setting.
