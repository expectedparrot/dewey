Published 2026 version of Barari et al.'s four randomized web-survey experiments with roughly 1,800 U.S. panel participants, comparing standardized questions with LLM-enabled confirmation and elaboration/relevance probes. Probing produced more detailed and informative open-ended responses; live coding was moderately accurate but showed acquiescence-related false positives, while respondent experience declined slightly. This is the same underlying study as src_2a843bf69441 and is retained as a publication-version artifact rather than independent evidence.
