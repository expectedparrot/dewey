Introduces SimSensei Kiosk/Ellie, a fully automated embodied virtual interviewer designed to conduct 15–25 minute semi-structured health interviews and collect verbal and nonverbal distress indicators. The paper documents an iterative path from human-interaction analysis through Wizard-of-Oz prototyping to an automatic dialogue system and reports a feasibility user study. It is foundational design evidence, but does not establish clinical effectiveness or population-level diagnostic accuracy.
