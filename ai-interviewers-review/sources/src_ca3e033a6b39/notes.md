# Detailed notes

## Design and contribution

Presents SimSensei Kiosk/Ellie, a fully automated embodied virtual interviewer for 15--25 minute semi-structured health interviews and collection of verbal and nonverbal distress indicators. Development proceeded from analysis of human interviews, through a Wizard-of-Oz prototype, to a fully automatic system integrating speech recognition, dialogue policy, and nonverbal behavior.

The evaluation compares the automatic system with its Wizard-of-Oz predecessor and reports feasibility and user engagement. The main contribution is system architecture and iterative design rather than a definitive estimate of diagnostic accuracy or population-level interview effects.

## Assessment

Foundational direct evidence on automated interviewer construction. Particularly useful for the review's design theme: standardization, replicability, multimodal sensing, rapport behaviors, and semi-structured follow-up. Claims about clinical decision support should remain separate from evidence that the system itself can sustain an interview; the study does not establish clinical effectiveness.

Full text: AAMAS 2014, pp. 1061--1068.
