Evaluates an AI-powered conversational survey against a conventional Qualtrics survey in a field study of roughly 600 participants and more than 5,200 free-text responses. The chatbot condition increased engagement and produced responses rated more informative, relevant, specific, and clear, including 39% more information and 25.7% higher aggregate response quality. This is a foundational large-sample predecessor to LLM interviewers, but it evaluates a proprietary pre-LLM conversational system and relies on Gricean-style response-quality metrics rather than broader qualitative validity.
