Introduces and evaluates AI-led qualitative interviews across economic applications. The paper argues that dynamic probing produces thematically richer responses than alternative scalable survey formats, can reveal otherwise unobserved themes, and yields responses associated with later behavior; it also studies hypothesis generation and thematic saturation. This is a central comparison paper because it makes strong validation claims using several quality criteria, complementing the more cautious richness findings in Cuevas et al.; the precise experimental comparisons and outcome definitions warrant close reading.
