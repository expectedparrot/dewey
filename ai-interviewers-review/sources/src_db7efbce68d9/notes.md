# Detailed notes

## Design and evidence

The paper demonstrates construction of a rule-based Landbot conversational survey and reports a 12-question chatbot survey completed by 200 Serbian respondents aged 18--55. It is primarily a design demonstration plus descriptive user-attitude survey, not a randomized comparison of chatbot and conventional interviewing.

The paper reports that 87% knew what a chatbot was, 44% had previously used one, and 88% preferred waiting for a human agent rather than receiving help from a chatbot. The preference item concerns service assistance and should not be treated as a direct estimate of preference for research interviews.

## Assessment

Useful as an accessible early implementation example and evidence that conversational presentation alone does not overcome trust and social-capability concerns. Evidentiary strength is limited by unclear sampling language, one national convenience-like sample, descriptive analysis, a rule-based rather than generative system, and the lack of a conventional-survey control group.

Full text: pp. 322--327, especially method/results on pp. 325--326.
