Describes a rule-based Landbot conversational survey and a descriptive study of 200 Serbian respondents. Although 87% knew what a chatbot was, only 44% had used one and 88% preferred waiting for a human service agent; the latter is not a direct research-interview preference measure. Useful as an early implementation and trust/UX case, but limited by unclear sampling, descriptive analysis, no conventional-survey control, and a rule-based rather than generative interviewer.
