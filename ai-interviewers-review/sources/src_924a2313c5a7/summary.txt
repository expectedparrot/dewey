Builds a hybrid rule-based and data-driven interview chatbot with active-listening capabilities and compares it with a baseline chatbot across four topics in a live study with 206 users. Active listening improved engagement and elicited higher-quality responses, offering concrete evidence that acknowledgment, comprehension, and adaptive follow-up behavior matter for automated interviewing. The study predates modern LLMs and tests a bounded feature set, making it most useful as design evidence rather than a direct estimate of current LLM interviewer performance.
